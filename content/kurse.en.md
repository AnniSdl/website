Living with pain takes a lot of strength. Often, much of it revolves around finally getting rid of the pain. This course, by contrast, is about how you can find your way back into your life – step by step. Over eight weeks, in a regular group and a considerate setting, you get to know mindfulness and compassion as tools for your everyday life with pain and stress.

## What is Mindfulness-Based Pain Management?

Mindfulness-Based Pain Management (MBPM) was developed by Vidyamala Burch. She has lived with chronic pain for many years and founded the organisation Breathworks in the UK in 2001, which has continued to develop the course and train teachers ever since.

The course is structured similarly to the well-known mindfulness programme MBSR, but is tailored specifically to people living with pain, illness or fatigue. MBPM combines mindfulness and compassion with knowledge from current neuroscientific research.

A core idea of MBPM is the distinction between two levels: the symptom itself and what often comes on top of it – tension, worry, brooding and the inner struggle against it. This second level is human and understandable, but it can add to the suffering. In the course, you practise noticing both levels mindfully and meeting what comes on top with more kindness.

## What to expect

- **Mindfulness:** You notice the body, the breath and sensations kindly and without judgement
- **Compassion:** You practise a kinder way of relating to yourself – especially in difficult moments
- **Gentle movement:** You do mindful movement exercises, adapted to what is possible for you today
- **Pacing:** You learn to balance activity and rest so that you can use your energy well
- **Knowledge:** You receive clearly explained basics about pain and the nervous system
- **Exchange:** You share experiences with people who know similar things
- **Daily practice:** You do guided meditations so that the practice finds its way into your everyday life

## Course structure

- **8 sessions** of 2.5 hours, once a week
- **Group size:** 12 to 15 participants
- **Course materials and audio recordings** for practice at home
- **No previous experience needed** – you can take part even if you have never meditated.
- The courses are held in German.

## A considerate space

There is no pressure to perform. Breaks and changes of position are always welcome. You can sit or move in whatever way feels good for you. Pacing, your individual limits and self-determination come first. We are happy to talk beforehand about whether the course format suits you and what you might need in order to take part.

## Who is it for?

For adults with persistent pain or other chronic symptoms such as tinnitus or fatigue – and for people in stressful life situations who are looking for a more mindful way of dealing with them.
