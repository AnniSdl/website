Eight weeks, a small group and a considerate setting: in the course you get to know mindfulness and compassion as tools for everyday life with pain and stress.

## What is Mindfulness-Based Pain Management?

Mindfulness-Based Pain Management (MBPM) was developed by Vidyamala Burch. She has lived with chronic pain for many years and founded the organisation Breathworks in the UK in 2001, which has continued to develop the course and train teachers ever since.

The course is structured similarly to the well-known mindfulness programme MBSR, but is tailored specifically to people living with pain, illness or fatigue. MBPM combines mindfulness and compassion with knowledge from pain research.

## What to expect

- **Mindfulness:** noticing the body, the breath and sensations kindly and without judgement
- **Compassion:** practising a kinder way of relating to yourself – especially in difficult moments
- **Gentle movement:** mindful movement adapted to what is possible for you today
- **Pacing:** balancing activity and rest so that you can use your energy well
- **Knowledge:** clearly explained basics about pain and the nervous system
- **Exchange:** sharing experiences with people who know similar things
- **Practice at home:** guided meditations to listen to, so the practice finds its way into your everyday life

## A considerate space

There is no pressure to perform. Breaks and changes of position are always welcome. With ME/CFS, pacing, your individual limits and self-determination come first. We are happy to clarify beforehand whether the course format suits you.

## Who is it for?

For adults with persistent pain or other chronic symptoms such as tinnitus or fatigue – and for people in stressful life situations who are looking for a more mindful way of dealing with them.
