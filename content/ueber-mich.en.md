I accompany people with chronic pain and other persistent symptoms – in individual counselling and in mindfulness courses. In my work, I combine sound knowledge, mindful practice and my own experience.

## My path

I know from my own experience how much chronic pain can restrict your life. For years, it shaped my everyday life. On my way out, Mindfulness-Based Pain Management and Pain Reprocessing Therapy helped me decisively. This experience is an important part of my motivation – and at the same time I know: every path is different.

## Training

- Mindfulness-Based Pain Management (MBPM) as taught by Vidyamala Burch – Breathworks, 2023
- Pain Reprocessing Therapy (PRT) as taught by Alan Gordon and Dr Howard Schubiner – Pain Reprocessing Therapy Center, 2026
- Regular mindfulness practice and self-exploration

## How I work

I would like to support you in finding your own steps back into a self-determined life. You set the pace, your limits come first, and breaks are always welcome. It is important to me that you get to know tools you can use yourself in your everyday life – and that you feel strengthened in your self-efficacy along the way.

You can find out more about [counselling](/beratung) and the [courses](/kurse).

My services are educational and preventive. They do not replace medical, psychotherapeutic or physiotherapeutic diagnosis or treatment.
